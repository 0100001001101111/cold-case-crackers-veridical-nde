# COLD CASE REG-07 — One strong veridical NDE case per four hundred, and its name is the crackers

## The case
The decisive test of whether anything leaves the body during these experiences is veridical perception: information acquired that the sleeping brain could not have had. The lab hand-graded every 'verification' claim in ~400 bundle narratives. Exactly ONE strong case survived: a reporter who floated to the hallway, saw her brother asleep with crackers, and whose mother independently asked about the crackers next morning - information-before-confirmation, single household detail.

## How it died
As a class: 36 of 39 graded claims were regex noise, practice narratives, or belief posts; one medium case had a mundane audio channel; the registry kill is formal (no verifiable case in the archives at claim strength). If perception left the skull, verified detail would be the rule, not a 1-in-400 rounding error - so the exit-model reading was NOT SUPPORTED in these archives - a bounded corpus result, not a universal verdict.

## What still stands
The one strong case, preserved with its structure named: specific, previously-unknown detail; independent witness; info-before-confirmation. Plus the frozen grading rubric that killed the other 38. Ceiling: case documentation.

## What would revive it
Five independent cases with the crackers structure, across collections, would reopen the class per the frozen resurrection rule. Anyone holding documented cases - dated, witnessed, detail-specific - knows if they have one.
